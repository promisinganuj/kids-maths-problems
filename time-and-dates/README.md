# Time, Date and Day Problems

## Difficulty Guide

- `(*)` Foundational level (quick single-step reasoning)
- `(**)` Intermediate level (2-step or mixed-concept reasoning)
- `(***)` Challenge level (multi-step selective-school style problems)

## Question 1 (*)

Which day of the week will Sam's birthday fall on in 2023, if he was born on March 15th, and January 2nd of 2023 falls on a Monday?

### Answer

- [x] Wednesday
- [ ] Friday
- [ ] Monday
- [ ] Tuesday

### Explanation

To determine the day of the week on which Sam's birthday will fall in 2023, we can follow these steps:

Find the number of days between January 2nd and March 15th in 2023. To do this, we can count the number of days from January 2nd to January 31st (29 days), from February 1st to February 28th (28 days), and from March 1st to March 15th (15 days): 
29 + 28 + 15 = 72 days

Note here that we don't count the starting day. That's why the number of days in January is not 30 (30-2+1) but 29 (31-2).

Divide the number of days by 7 to get the number of full weeks between January 2nd and March 15th.
72 ÷ 7 = 10 weeks remainder 2 days

This means that there are 10 full weeks and 2 extra days between January 2nd and March 15th.

Add the extra days to Monday (the day of the week on January 2nd) to find the day of the week on March 15th.
Since 2 extra days have passed, the day of the week on March 15th will be two days after Monday, which is Wednesday.
Therefore, Sam's birthday will fall on a Wednesday in 2023.

## Question 2 (*)

If 1 January 2026 is a Thursday, what day of the week will 1 January 2027 be?

### Answer

- [ ] Wednesday
- [x] Friday
- [ ] Thursday
- [ ] Saturday

### Explanation

Year 2026 is not a leap year, so it has 365 days.

$365 \div 7$ leaves remainder $1$, so the weekday moves forward by 1 day.

If 1 January 2026 is Thursday, then 1 January 2027 will be Friday.

## Question 3 (*)

Today is Tuesday. What day will it be 100 days from today?

### Answer

- [ ] Monday
- [ ] Tuesday
- [ ] Wednesday
- [x] Thursday

### Explanation

$100 \div 7 = 14$ remainder $2$.

So, the day moves forward by 2 days from Tuesday.

Tuesday + 2 days = Thursday.

## Question 4 (**)

A train leaves Melbourne at 9:45 PM and arrives in Sydney at 7:20 AM the next day (local time, same time zone for this problem). How long is the journey?

### Answer

- [ ] 8 hours 45 minutes
- [x] 9 hours 35 minutes
- [ ] 10 hours 15 minutes
- [ ] 9 hours 25 minutes

### Explanation

From 9:45 PM to 12:00 midnight is 2 hours 15 minutes.

From midnight to 7:20 AM is 7 hours 20 minutes.

Total duration = 2:15 + 7:20 = 9 hours 35 minutes.

## Question 5 (**)

If 29 February 2024 is a Thursday, what day of the week will 1 March 2024 be?

### Answer

- [ ] Thursday
- [x] Friday
- [ ] Saturday
- [ ] Wednesday

### Explanation

The day after any given day is the next weekday.

So, the day after Thursday is Friday.

Hence, 1 March 2024 is Friday.

## Question 6 (**)

Riya starts homework at 4:35 PM. She studies for 1 hour 45 minutes, takes a break of 20 minutes, and then studies for another 55 minutes. At what time does she finish?

### Answer

- [ ] 7:15 PM
- [ ] 7:25 PM
- [x] 7:35 PM
- [ ] 7:45 PM

### Explanation

Total time spent = 1 hour 45 minutes + 20 minutes + 55 minutes

= 1 hour + (45 + 20 + 55) minutes

= 1 hour + 120 minutes

= 3 hours.

Start time 4:35 PM + 3 hours = 7:35 PM.

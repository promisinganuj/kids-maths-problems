# Profit and Loss Problems

## Difficulty Guide

- `(*)` Foundational level (quick single-step reasoning)
- `(**)` Intermediate level (2-step or mixed-concept reasoning)
- `(***)` Challenge level (multi-step selective-school style problems)

## Question 1 (*)

A shopkeeper buys a calculator for $48 and sells it for $60. What is the profit percentage?

### Answer

- [ ] 20%
- [x] 25%
- [ ] 22.5%
- [ ] 30%

### Explanation

Profit = $60 - $48 = $12.

Profit percentage = $(12/48) \times 100 = 25\%$.

## Question 2 (*)

A sports store marked a football at $80. During a sale, it gave a 15% discount. If the cost price of the football was $62, what was the profit or loss?

### Answer

- [x] Profit of $6
- [ ] Profit of $8
- [ ] Loss of $6
- [ ] Loss of $8

### Explanation

Selling price after discount = $80 - 15\%$ of $80 = $80 - $12 = $68.

Profit = $68 - $62 = $6.

Therefore, the correct option is profit of $6.

## Question 3 (*)

A laptop is sold at a 20% profit. If its selling price is $1,080, what is its cost price?

### Answer

- [ ] $840
- [ ] $880
- [x] $900
- [ ] $920

### Explanation

If profit is 20%, then selling price is 120% of cost price.

So, cost price = $1080 / 1.2 = $900.

## Question 4 (**)

A jacket has a marked price of $150. It is sold with two successive discounts: 10% and then 20%. What is the final selling price?

### Answer

- [ ] $105
- [ ] $112
- [x] $108
- [ ] $120

### Explanation

After first 10% discount: $150 \times 0.9 = $135.

After second 20% discount: $135 \times 0.8 = $108.

## Question 5 (**)

A store buys a study lamp for $55 (GST exclusive) and marks it up by 40% before adding 10% GST to the marked price. What is the final price paid by the customer?

### Answer

- [ ] $77.00
- [ ] $80.85
- [x] $84.70
- [ ] $88.00

### Explanation

Marked price before GST = $55 \times 1.4 = $77.

Final price after 10% GST = $77 \times 1.1 = $84.70.

## Question 6 (**)

A retailer marks a backpack 25% above cost price. During a promotion, a 10% discount is offered on the marked price. If the final selling price is $135, what was the cost price?

### Answer

- [ ] $108
- [ ] $115
- [ ] $118
- [x] $120

### Explanation

Let cost price be $C$.

Marked price = $1.25C$.

After 10% discount, selling price = $1.25C \times 0.9 = 1.125C$.

Given $1.125C = 135$, so $C = 135/1.125 = 120$.

## Question 7 (***)

A watch was sold for $184 after two successive discounts of 20% and 8% on the marked price. What was the marked price?

### Answer

- [ ] $230
- [x] $250
- [ ] $240
- [ ] $260

### Explanation

After discounts, selling price = Marked price $\times 0.8 \times 0.92 = \text{Marked price} \times 0.736$.

So, marked price = $184 / 0.736 = 250$.

## Question 8 (***)

A bookseller buys 50 identical notebooks at $6 each. He sells 30 notebooks at a 25% profit and the remaining 20 notebooks at a 10% loss. What is his overall profit or loss?

### Answer

- [ ] Profit of $24
- [ ] Loss of $6
- [x] Profit of $33
- [ ] Profit of $18

### Explanation

Total cost = $50 \times 6 = $300.

For 30 notebooks: selling price per notebook = $6 \times 1.25 = $7.50, so total = $225.

For 20 notebooks: selling price per notebook = $6 \times 0.9 = $5.40, so total = $108.

Total selling price = $225 + $108 = $333.

Overall profit = $333 - $300 = $33.

## Question 9 (***)

A gaming headset is sold for $198 including 10% GST. The retailer’s cost price (excluding GST) is $150. What is the retailer’s profit percentage (on cost price, excluding GST)?

### Answer

- [ ] 18%
- [x] 20%
- [ ] 21%
- [ ] 22%

### Explanation

Selling price excluding GST = $198 / 1.1 = $180.

Profit = $180 - $150 = $30.

Profit percentage = $(30/150) \times 100 = 20\%$.

## Question 10 (***)

A merchant first increases the marked price of an item by 20% and then gives a 20% discount on that new marked price. Compared with the original marked price, what is the final percentage change in price?

### Answer

- [ ] No change
- [ ] 2% increase
- [x] 4% decrease
- [ ] 4% increase

### Explanation

Assume original marked price is $100.

After 20% increase, new marked price = $120.

After 20% discount, final price = $120 \times 0.8 = $96.

So, the price is $4$ less than $100$, i.e., a 4% decrease.
